A few things worth knowing

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Is my business a good fit?+

We work with local service businesses such as barbers, plumbers and salons. If you have a service people can understand, a clear service area and capacity for more work, let’s talk about whether a test makes sense.

What does the R1,500 cover?+

The R1,500 goes into advertising for the initial test. We discuss the creative, campaign scope and any service payment with you before you start.

How do you get paid?+

We agree the amount and payment method directly with you. There is no fixed service price listed here. Your advertising budget and our service payment are discussed separately so you understand the full investment.

Do I have to continue after the test?+

No pressure to continue. We review the test together and you decide what comes next. If you continue, you choose your advertising investment and agree the service payment with us.

Can you guarantee bookings?+

No. Results depend on your market, offer, budget and how enquiries are handled. The test helps us learn what works for your business. Read the terms of service before starting.
