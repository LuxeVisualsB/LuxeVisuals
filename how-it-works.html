Before we begin

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Knowing what you’re agreeing to matters. We’ll talk through the details with you.

Is R1 500 your service fee?

No. R1 500 is the starting money used for advertising. The payment amount and method for our service are discussed directly with you. Confirm both before you pay.

Do I have to keep advertising after the test?

The test is a starting point. We review the results together, and you decide whether to continue and how much to invest in advertising next.

What does my test include?

We discuss the photography, advertising, and test scope with you before starting. The first conversation is where we establish what your business needs and what work is included.

Can you guarantee new clients?

No. We do not guarantee any specific results, sales, or number of clients. The test helps you make an informed decision, but advertising outcomes can vary.

What should I have ready?

Your business name, the area you serve, and what you want more of: enquiries, bookings, or online sales. If you have a website or social page, send that too. You don’t need a finished marketing brief.
